# Welcome to The Hanged Man

A Dragon Age fan collective — and, since August 2026, a Swedish non-profit
association in formation (under the name The Hanged Man Club) — built for adults
who like their fandom thoughtful: deep meta, honest lore debates, creative
fanworks, and events run by the community, for the community. Pull up a chair —
the coffee's dark, the drinks are strong, and someone's always ready to talk
Thedas.

## Who We Are

We're a creator-friendly corner of the Dragon Age fandom, and formally, a
non-profit association (_ideell förening_) whose purpose is to support, promote,
and facilitate creative fan communities and collaborative projects.
Non-political, non-commercial, and built to last past any one person's
involvement — that's the structure underneath the tavern doors.

In practice, that means deep meta and lore debates, fanworks of every kind,
collaborative exchanges and events, and good-faith conversation that makes a
fandom feel like home.

Share your latest fic. Scream about your blorbo. Join an exchange. Or just lurk
with a mug of something warm — there's a place at the table either way.
