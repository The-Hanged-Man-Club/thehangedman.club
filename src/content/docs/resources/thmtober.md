themed challenge hosted by The Hanged Man. With one prompt per day, sketch, line
art, paint, drabble, flash fic, meta theorize, or research to your heart's
content and share whatever you worked on with _#THMtober_. Be sure to
**@TheHangedManClub** on BlueSky or Tumblr and we'll share your works! You can
also join the fun in our Discord.
